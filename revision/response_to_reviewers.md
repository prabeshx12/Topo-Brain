## Reviewer 1

### R1.1 — VGG perceptual loss in training vs rejecting VGG/ImageNet (LPIPS) for evaluation
**[DONE, writing] + [PENDING-GPU, robustness].** We agree the original wording was
contradictory and have corrected it (§3.4). We now draw the distinction explicitly: a reported
perceptual *metric* (LPIPS) is read as a calibrated measure of fidelity, so an uncalibrated
VGG/ImageNet space could mislead; a weak *training regulariser* need only supply a useful
gradient toward plausible texture and does not require calibration. The VGG16 term carries
weight 0.25, is warmed in only after iteration 12k, and contributes a small fraction of the
objective. As a robustness check we will additionally report a variant in which the perceptual
term is replaced by a medical-image-pretrained feature extractor (RadImageNet/MedicalNet); the
swap is implemented and queued. **[PENDING-GPU]**

### R1.2 — No SOTA baselines, only self-ablation
**[PENDING-GPU].** We are adding quantitative and qualitative comparisons against (i) a
deterministic 3D regression U-Net (same backbone, diffusion removed), (ii) a 3D GAN/pix2pix
baseline, and, subject to public-code availability, a domain method (WATNet/VCT). Training
wrappers and the evaluation harness (SSIM/PSNR/Dice/HD95 + true topological metrics) are
prepared; runs are queued pending GPU. We report this explicitly rather than claim superiority
prematurely.

### R1.3 — Ablation too coarse; isolate Sobel and Dice
**[DONE, writing] + [PENDING-GPU].** First, we corrected the loss description (§3.3): the term
is a multi-scale **edge-aware boundary regulariser** = edge-weighted cross-entropy + a boundary
Dice on Sobel edges, *not* the gradient-CE + volumetric-Dice of the previous equation. We have
added configuration flags isolating (a) edge-weighted-CE only, (b) boundary-Dice only, and
(c) single- vs multi-scale, and will report the resulting finer ablation table. **[PENDING-GPU]**

### R1.4 — 9 train / 1 test, large 3D model → overfitting
**[DONE, writing] + [PENDING-GPU/data].** We note the model is **14.0 M** parameters
(13,978,821; the previous "22.4 M" was a documentation error, now corrected and verified by a
released script), smaller than stated. To address evaluation size we (i) implement leave-one-
subject-out (LOSO) cross-validation on the 10 paired subjects and report mean ± SD across folds,
and (ii) add an **independent external paired cohort** (Li et al. 2025, 20 subjects, different
site/scanner) for additional held-out synthesis evaluation. LOSO runs are queued. **[PENDING-GPU]**

### R1.5 — Weak downstream (synth AUC 0.653 vs native-3T K-means 0.818)
**[DONE, writing] + [PENDING-CKPT].** We have made this comparison statistically honest (§4.6).
With a leave-one-subject-out reference and stratified bootstrap CIs, the synthetic composite
gives AUC 0.653 (95% CI [0.44, 0.84]) and the unsupervised native-3T K-means composite 0.809
(95% CI [0.64, 0.94]); the intervals overlap and the difference is **not significant**
(DeLong p = 0.073, n = 30). The gap is therefore not evidence that synthesis discards diagnostic
signal — at four-class tissue resolution AD atrophy is already largely separable, so neither
score tests the synthesis hypothesis. The mechanistic test is finer parcellation; we are running
FastSurfer hippocampal-subfield features on synthesised vs native volumes (DeLong comparison),
queued pending the trained checkpoint. **[PENDING-CKPT]**

**[DONE, writing] + [PENDING-CKPT].** See R1.5: at coarse granularity the difference is not

### R2.5 — Generalisation beyond the single training scanner/protocol
**[DONE, writing] + [PENDING-CKPT].** The ADNI deployment is already an out-of-distribution,
multi-scanner cohort. We additionally add the independent Li et al. 2025 paired 3T/7T cohort
(different site and scanner from the UNC training data) for quantitative cross-cohort synthesis
metrics. Inference queued pending the checkpoint. **[PENDING-CKPT]**

### R2.6 — Justify conditional diffusion over GAN / latent diffusion / transformer / regression
**[DONE].** We added an explicit design-rationale paragraph (§5, "Why conditional diffusion?")
covering the failure modes of deterministic regression (oversmoothing, no anatomy hook), GANs
(mode collapse, unfaithful hallucination), latent diffusion (latent encoder discards voxel-grid
detail), and 3D transformers (poor scaling), and why conditional diffusion uniquely exposes a
per-step target for coupling the segmentation head and boundary regulariser. The baseline
experiments (R1.2) substantiate this empirically.

### R2.7 — Single hold-out vs k-fold/LOSO; stable across folds? (cf. R1.4)
**[PENDING-GPU].** Addressed by the LOSO harness (R1.4); we will report per-fold metrics and
their dispersion.

### R2.8 — Compare loss with persistent-homology / topology-aware Dice / clDice / Euler losses
**[DONE, writing] + [PENDING].** We have reframed our term honestly as an edge-aware boundary
approximation (not a persistent-homology loss) and now evaluate preservation with true
topological metrics (R2.3). A direct comparison of our regulariser against clDice and a
persistent-homology loss is identified as the principal subject of follow-up work; where compute
permits we will include at least one such comparison in this revision.
