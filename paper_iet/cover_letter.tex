\opening{Dear Editor,}

We submit for your consideration the manuscript entitled
\textbf{``TopoBrain: Anatomy-Aware Conditional Diffusion for 3T-to-7T
MRI Synthesis with Application to Alzheimer's Disease Biomarkers''}
for publication in \textit{IET Image Processing} as a research
article.

\textit{Why this work fits IET Image Processing.} The manuscript
presents an anatomy-aware conditional diffusion framework that
synthesises 7-Tesla T1-weighted MRI from routine 3-Tesla acquisitions
on the same voxel grid, with explicit segmentation supervision and an
edge-aware topology loss. The work sits squarely within the journal's
scope (deep learning for medical image enhancement, super-resolution,
generative models, segmentation) and contributes both methodological
novelty (the dual-decoder topology-regularised diffusion architecture)
and a complete validated downstream evaluation pipeline.

\textit{Key contributions and findings.}
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep1pt
\item \textbf{Architecture:} a 22.4-million-parameter 3D U-Net with
joint noise-prediction and tissue-segmentation decoders, trained with
an edge-aware topology loss that explicitly regularises tissue
boundaries.
\item \textbf{Held-out paired evaluation:} SSIM\,$=$\,0.8991,
PSNR\,$=$\,20.00\,dB, brain-mask Dice\,$=$\,0.9436 and
HD95\,$=$\,2.05\,mm.
\item \textbf{Topology-loss ablation:} 6.5$\times$ reduction in
whole-brain HD95 (13.34\,mm $\rightarrow$ 2.05\,mm) and substantial
reduction in tissue-class fragmentation across CSF, GM, and WM.
\item \textbf{Subcortical fidelity for AD:} bilateral hippocampi,
amygdalae, thalami, and caudates recovered as essentially single
connected components (largest-CC fraction $\geq$\,99.8\,\%).
\item \textbf{Out-of-distribution validation:} 30 ADNI baseline 3T
subjects (15 AD, 15 CN) yield anatomically coherent synthesis;
six-of-six tissue-volume biomarker directions match Alzheimer's
literature, with CSF/GM atrophy index Cohen's $d=+0.681$.
\end{enumerate}

\textit{Reproducibility.} All code, configurations, training
recipes, evaluation scripts and analysis tooling are openly available
at \url{https://github.com/prabeshx12/Topo-Brain}. The pipeline is
self-validated against synthetic data with known signal-to-noise
properties.

\textit{Originality.} The manuscript has not been published or
submitted for publication elsewhere. All authors have read and
approved the submission. We declare no conflict of interest. ADNI
data are used under the standard ADNI Data Use Agreement; we comply
with all redistribution restrictions and do not redistribute the raw
imaging data.

\textit{Suggested reviewers.} If helpful for assignment, the editorial
team may consider experts in: (i) medical-image super-resolution and
domain translation, (ii) topology-aware segmentation, and
(iii) Alzheimer's neuroimaging biomarkers.

We thank you in advance for considering our submission and look
forward to the reviewers' feedback.
