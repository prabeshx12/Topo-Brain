7-Tesla MRI improves Alzheimer's biomarker measurement but is rarely
available clinically; almost all routine scans are at 3-Tesla. We
present TopoBrain, a deep-learning model that synthesises 7T-quality
images from a routine 3T scan on the patient's own voxel grid,
preserving the brain structures most relevant to early Alzheimer's
detection. Across paired 3T/7T validation and an out-of-distribution
ADNI cohort, the model produces anatomically faithful outputs whose
tissue-volume measurements reproduce six of six expected
Alzheimer's-disease biomarker directions, opening the way to
7T-quality biomarker analysis on standard 3T hardware.
